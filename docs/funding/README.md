## The strategy in six lines

1. **Equity-free money NOW** (grants, hackathons, competitions) — needs no traction story, no entity.
2. **The pilot's carve-out data is the master key** — angels/VC come AFTER it exists, not before.
3. **Incorporate (Delaware C-corp) before any SAFE** — no entity = no check can close. (~$500–1,200; SBS offers free legal consults.)
4. **Immigrant-founder VC (Unshackled, One Way) is the best-matched first-check tier** — pre-traction is their thesis.
5. **CUNY/Brooklyn competitions land Oct 2026–Spring 2027** — the honest "real money" timeline is the school year.
6. Odds estimates are judgment, not data. The only number that matters: **one angel's pre-stated "if you show me X, I write $Y."**
