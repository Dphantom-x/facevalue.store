## Bucket 4 — Brooklyn / NYC

- **BPL PowerUP — highest-fit pure-cash competition.** $20K/$10K/$5K (verified on powerup.smapply.io);
  18+, Brooklyn resident, *planning* to start a business in Brooklyn — **pre-entity, idea-stage
  friendly**; mandatory orientation first. 2026 closed (May 4); next ~spring 2027. Prize needs a W-9.
  Prep = the SBS FastTrac plan.
- **NYCEDC Founder Fellowship** — next window **~Oct–Dec 2026** (2026 cohort running since March).
  Hard gates: tech startup, ≤2 founders, ≥1 NYC-resident founder. "Underrepresented" is encouraged,
  not gated — Hispanic/first-gen is squarely the intended profile. **Pick the Visible Hands (VHNYC)
  track = $10,000 non-dilutive** (verified on visiblehands.vc/program-vhnyc); Company Ventures = 0
  equity, no cash; Newlab ≈ investment (dilutive). Watch edc.nyc/founder-fellowship from October.
- **NYC SBS** — **FastTrac NewVenture**: free 10-session founder bootcamp, no entity needed
  (nyc-business.nyc.gov → FastTrac) — builds the PowerUP-ready plan. **Free legal consults** — book one
  the week of incorporation. Post-entity: **M/WBE certification** (Hispanic-owned) opens
  supplier-diversity channels.
