## Approach etiquette
- **Promoters/venues:** 5-sentence email to the *exact* functional address (booking vs events vs partnerships); subject like *"Held-back 30 tickets, verified fans, zero cost to you — [venue]"*; frame it as **their** experiment (their brand, their DICE listing untouched, you run the code-gated carve-out + hand them funnel data). **Never say "bot," never propose resale.** Follow up once after ~a week, then IG DM referencing the email.
- **Collectives:** IG DM / form, peer tone, lead with scene-protection ("keep tickets with the community"), offer co-branding + door data — not "partner with a startup."
- **Press:** one-line news hook + one paragraph + link. Local outlets want the human story; trade outlets want data + the FTC/DOJ resonance.
- **World/TFH:** ship on their rails first (a World ID integration/mini-app), *then* email music@toolsforhumanity.com with a working demo + pilot numbers.
