# FaceValue — Fundraising Playbook

> **Purpose:** a self-contained guide to raising money for FaceValue — grants, non-dilutive
> competitions, student VC, and angels — with real application details and sources, so a fresh
> Claude chat (or future-you) can pick this up cold and start applying.
>
> **Researched 2026-06-22** via multiple web-research passes (sources at the bottom). **This is a
> point-in-time snapshot.** Programs, amounts, and deadlines change — **verify on the live page before
> relying on any number or date.** Flagged uncertainties are marked ⚠️. Probability estimates are
> informed judgment, not data.

  layer. FaceValue's wedge is device-level (no Orb), bottom-up, NYC-native. (See
  `docs/BUSINESS.md` / memory.)

### The one idea that governs everything below
**The pilot's verify-rate number is the master key.** It moves angel odds from ~7% to ~35%, makes
you a top-quartile grant/competition applicant, and gives the Valiron intro something concrete to say.
**Fundraising is not separate from the pilot — the pilot IS the fundraising foundation.** So: bank the
small equity-free wins now (they compound credibility), but the serious checks follow the number.

### Non-dilutive vs. dilutive (know which you're taking)
- **Non-dilutive = free money** (you give up nothing): grants, hackathon prizes, most competitions.
- **Dilutive = you sell equity** (a SAFE): Dorm Room Fund, angels, accelerators. At pre-seed this is
  normal and fine from the *right* source — just know the difference.

## 3. ANGELS — the playbook (after the pilot shows a number)

**Honest odds of a small check ($5K–$50K):** **~30–40% WITH warm intros + a pilot number, within 3–6
months. ~5–10% cold or pre-data.** The pilot number is what moves you between those worlds.

### The doors that fit pre-revenue
| Door | Why | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Dorm Room Fund** | Student-only; you qualify. Most accessible serious check. | https://www.dormroomfund.com/apply (also in §1) |
| **37 Angels** | **Explicitly pre-revenue-friendly.** Network deploys $50K–$200K; ~$25K typical individual check. | Gust app → fit call → pitch forum; apply ≥2 wks before a forum. https://www.37angels.com/founders · admin@37angels.com ⚠️ *stale-looking site; confirm next forum by email* |
| **HBS Alumni Angels of Greater NY** | ~300 investors (largest in the Northeast). **You do NOT need to be an HBS alum to pitch.** | Open Gust app → monthly pitch night (first Monday). https://www.hbsangelsny.com/apply-to-pitch |
| **New York Angels** *(later)* | Open application, no warm intro needed — BUT seed-leaning (~$500K median, wants traction). Revisit *with* pilot data. | https://www.newyorkangels.com/ · /investment-process |
| **TiE New York** *(later)* | Relationship-driven; on-ramp is attending TiE NY events (open to non-members). | https://ny.tie.org/ |

### First-check norms (so you don't over-ask)
- **Size:** $10K–$50K per angel, **~$25–35K typical.** A sub-$500K round stacks 8–20 angels.
- **Instrument:** **YC post-money SAFE, valuation-cap-only** (~92% of pre-seed use SAFEs; ~85% post-money;
  ~⅔ cap-only).
- **Valuation cap (US pre-seed, 2025–26):** under-$250K raise → ~$7.5M median; $250–500K → ~$10M. For
  **pre-revenue-with-a-pilot, anchor ~$5–10M post-money** (the pilot justifies the upper half). Don't
  over-ask the cap — an unrealistic cap makes an angel walk.

### ⭐ Converting the Valiron relationship into intros (the highest-leverage move)
Warm intros convert **~13× better than cold** (DocSend). The norm is **double opt-in** — never ask for a
blind intro. Sequence, easiest first:
1. **Let them do the public feature first** (the X shoutout they already offered) → that's social proof
   you cite in every other email.
2. **Ask for ONE specific intro** — not "investors you know," but *"who are the 1–2 people in your network
   who back pre-seed consumer/identity startups? Would you forward a short blurb?"* — and hand them a
   **ready-to-forward, <150-word email** they can send in 15 seconds:
   - Subject: `Aldo (FaceValue) <> [Investor] ([Fund]) | intro?`
   - Body: one line on *why this investor* (their thesis), **traction in numbers** (the pilot —
     named venue, verify-rate, users), one line on the round (size/stage), one ask (a 20-min call in 2
     weeks, 2 time options). **Link the deck, never attach.**
3. **Then ask them for a token angel check themselves** — a funded founder writing even a little de-risks
   you for the next angel.

### What to SKIP (for now)
- **Cold AngelList** — it's investor/fund infrastructure, not a "post and get funded" door.
- **Equity crowdfunding (Wefunder / Republic / Reg CF)** — **~60% of offerings raise nothing**; it
  *monetizes a crowd you already have*, it doesn't create one. Revisit **after** the summer party builds a
  fan community. Keep any raise in the review tier (<~$1.235M) to avoid an audit.

## 5. DEAD-ENDS — don't waste time

- **World *Foundation* grants** (Spark/Scale/Dev Rewards) — **US residents/entities excluded** (verified).
- **Thiel Fellowship** — age cap **22** (you're ~23 → over) **and** requires dropping out. ⚠️ verify your
  exact age vs. their cutoff, but assume out.
- **Pioneer (pioneer.app)** — **defunct** since 2024.
- **No anti-scalping / ticketing-specific grant exists** (searched hard).
- **No standalone "proof-of-personhood" grant** — it's only a sub-theme inside World / Gitcoin / EF ESP.
- **GSEA** (Global Student Entrepreneur Awards) — needs **$500 revenue + 6 mo operating**; becomes a
  great fit *once FaceValue charges anyone*. https://eonetwork.org/gsea/
- **Affiliation-gated competitions:** Rice (needs a grad student), MIT $100K (MIT student), NYU Challenge
  (NYU-founded + dilutive KISS), Columbia VC (Columbia only), Cornell Tech Runway (needs a PhD), Hult
  Prize (needs a 2–4 person team).
- **Halcyon** — social-impact/climate/health only (poor thematic fit).
- **Tech:NYC** — network/credibility only, **no money** (still worth joining for visibility).
- **CUNY Tech Prep / CUNY 2X** — job training, not funding.

## 6. MATERIALS to prepare (the foundation that makes all of the above faster)

1. **A tight deck** (link, never attach): problem (scalpers beat fans) → why-now (World ID makes
   personhood enforceable + legal in NYC) → the wedge (zero-risk code-gate carve-out) → **the pilot
   number** (verify-rate, named venue) → the moat (verified-fan network + personhood standard) →
   ask/use-of-funds tied to a concrete next milestone → team + the Valiron/Concert-Kit context.
2. **A one-page data room / live metrics view** (the funnel: views → verified → claimed, trend across
   venues).
3. **The forwardable <150-word warm-intro email** (template in §3) — the load-bearing artifact for the
   Valiron lever.
4. **A 1517 Medici 5-min Loom script** — who you are, the passion, what the $1K unlocks.
5. **The Emergent Ventures ≤1,500-word proposal** — your story; one consensus view you agree with; the
   idea/problem/budget/timeline.

*(A fresh chat can draft any of these — ask it to.)*

## 8. Honest scale check

These are **$1K–$40K non-dilutive grants/prizes** plus a couple of equity first-checks ($25K angel,
$90–250K Dorm Room Fund). As a **portfolio across 2026–27**, they buy credibility, social proof, and
runway for a ≤$5K NYC pilot — **not** company-scale fundraising. Your real edge: a **working product +
hackathon wins** puts you above the typical idea-stage entrant in every pool. Lean into hackathons +
grants (solo-friendly, on-theme, rolling) while the annual competitions come back around — and remember
the master key: **land the pilot, produce the number, lead with it.**
